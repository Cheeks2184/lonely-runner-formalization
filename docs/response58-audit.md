## Route assessment

Prime-forcing/product induction is a genuine unrestricted fourth contract and
has the strongest connection to recent successful finite-dimensional work.
Its first unsupported implication is nevertheless severe: produce, uniformly
in the dimension, enough moduli satisfying a growing finite cover theorem to
exceed the product bound. The local property may encode nearly the original
covering problem.

The universal pair-sum certificate is a clean direct bridge and survives the
reported finite search, but it is visibly stronger than LRC and has no
mechanism yet forcing the required CRT overlaps. Top-two remains open. The
current signed-divisor replacement is too sparse to support height compression.

The response did not know the independently discovered DPLP bridge. The
project therefore retains DPLP as rank one pending one targeted Sol Pro
adversarial turn, adds prime-forcing as rank two, and ranks the mature
top-two/all-pivot route third. No route is promoted to a proof.
