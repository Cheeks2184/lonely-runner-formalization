# Forty-seventh Astra checkpoint: ingredients for the two-divisible branch

Three reusable declarations now have source proofs. A positive natural speed
at least five times every other labelled speed can be inserted to obtain a
positive sixth-margin witness. A finite anchored fiber admits a maximum of
the minimum of two norms, even when that maximum is zero. Three nondivisible
labels admit a common safe third shift whenever two distinct labels are bad
at the initial time. None requires distinct speed values.

## Fast speed and positive time

The fast-speed adapter applies the existing four-moving fifth-margin theorem
and quantitative insertion theorem. The selected speed is nonzero by positivity,
and natural casts discharge the absolute-value speed comparison. The resulting
time is nonzero because its selected coordinate has positive norm. If it is
negative, reflection gives a positive time with the same five norms. Equality
in the five-times speed threshold remains valid.

## A maximum that can equal zero

For a positive natural anchor speed c, normalized times with fractional anchor
phase 5/6 are exactly (j+5/6)/c for j in Fin c. The finite maximum theorem
selects one maximizing the minimum of the two requested circle norms. Every
real anchored time, including a negative one, reduces by integer periodicity
to this list. The floor of its normalized anchor product supplies the bounded
natural index. The chosen time lies strictly between zero and one.

The two objective speeds can be zero, and the maximum can be zero. This avoids
assuming the strict seed required by the earlier complement maximizer, whose
domain and objective are different. This is a symbolic finite-existence proof,
with no executed enumeration for a specific speed.

## Three exceptions with a shared bad shift

When two distinct labels are bad at shift zero, remove one of them. The
remaining family has at most two labels, so the checked common-shift theorem
supplies a safe shift. The other initially bad label forces that shift to be
nonzero. The removed label has at most one bad shift, already known to be zero,
so it too is safe. This reuses the checked separation theorem and retains the
strict badness threshold and closed sixth-margin conclusion. The labels form
an arbitrary finite type of cardinality at most three; repeated speeds and
phases are allowed.
