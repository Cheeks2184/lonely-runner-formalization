## Changing an original bounded relation

For n >= 6 positive distinct integer moving speeds, retain the actual
global maximum mu < alpha = 1/(n+1), lower-count witness supply, and the
previously accepted full-rank bound E = 2n^2(n(n+1)-1). Removing an
independent relation produces a rational plane in which the adjacent-ratio
construction gives an integer vector w with an opposite pair and no zero
coordinate. Lower-count supply gives all its original slots a common
closed beta = 1/n witness. At least one original bounded relation changes.

The finite-kernel comparison retains the full effect of those changes.
With the predecessor's kernel J, write a_r = product_i Jhat(r_i) and

    H_w(t) = integral_s product_i J(v_i s-w_i t).

At every complete gamma-good time of w, the original global maximum gives
H_w(t) <= T(gamma-mu), where
T(eta) = (8PM)^n / [4^P(M eta)^(2P)], M = n(n+1), P = 2n^2.
For gamma = beta this bound is strictly below one. The exact Fourier sum
also gives a quantitative exclusion of aligned defect phases at every
supplied witness. It supplies no witness violating that exclusion.

A scalar weight independent of all coordinate phase shifts can reproduce
the changed orbit's finite-kernel averages exactly if and only if the
new resonance indicator is constant on each original frequency fiber.
The zero vector and any changed bounded original relation violate this
condition. The manuscript and its independent review prove an explicit
positive error lower bound for that fixed kernel. This rejects the
universal identity; a comparison specialized to the unshifted good-time
predicate remains open. The error bound is not claimed to exceed the
tolerance required by any proposed approximate transfer.

## Local differential compensation

The separate slack-energy proposal concerns d >= 2 distinct positive
primitive integer speeds and 0 < a < alpha = 1/(d+1). Put
P(t) = product_i (||v_i t||-a)_+, I = integral P, and H = -dI/da.
The desired global inequality (d+1)I-(alpha-a)H >= 0 remains unproved
and unrefuted. Its previously reviewed implication to closed witness
existence does not supply this inequality.

The new calculation tests a particular certificate: subtract the full
distributional derivative of lambda P + nu P' from the defect density,
using bounded multipliers with finitely many regular pieces, and require
the remaining measure to be nonnegative. Every threshold atom, peak atom,
and multiplier jump must be retained. Both P and P' vanish near every
original integer-phase cut, so such a derivative has zero net mass in
each integer-phase cell.

For the actual good tuple (1,2,3), the cell [1/3,1/2] has negative defect
at every 0 < a < 1/5. The exact derivative atoms balance inside that cell.
Consequently this entire specified local certificate class cannot prove
the global inequality. The tuple has its native witness and positive
full defect; it is not a counterexample to the energy proposal or LRC.
Charging between separated cells by a genuinely nonlocal arithmetic rule
is outside the rejected class and still needs a substantive estimate.
