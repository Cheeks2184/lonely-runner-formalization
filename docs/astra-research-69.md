# Pass 69: the general slow-speed gap and exact ideal-row control

The accepted unit-speed cluster theorem does not yet extend to an arbitrary
integer slow speed. The general-slow audit and its independent review identify
two concrete failures of the attempted residue proof. The very same family
has another explicit forward witness, so those failures do not refute the
candidate construction.

The proposed contract has N>=5, one positive integer slow speed a and N-2
fast speeds M+c, with distinct nonnegative offsets containing zero,
H=max c, and M>(N-1)max(H,a). Its proposed times are
k/(Na)+ell/(NM), with both digits between zero and N-1. The slow-admissible
closed pairs are exactly rows k=1,...,N-2 and the exceptional pair (N-1,0).
The latter is absent from the stronger forward target.

For every N>=5 and integer a>N, the family M=Na-1 and offsets
{0,...,N-4,a} shows that the row floors are not linear residues and that a
favorable floored residue can acquire a carry across the closed upper face.
Nevertheless its assignment (k,ell)=(1,2) is forward-good for every label.
The audit's coupled row inequalities retain the actual common time and leave
the general selection obligation explicit.

## Testing the ideal selection mechanism

The zero-correction phase model is (k r_i+ell)/N, with the same slopes in
every row. A root manuscript proves a one-way conditional transfer: any
strict failure on all slow-admissible closed pairs at fixed rational slopes
produces an infinite family of actual integer cluster failures, with separation
ratio tending to infinity. A common denominator q gives slow speed q and
fast speeds NqT+d_i. The correction is bounded by H(N-1)/(NM), so a positive
finite bad margin survives for sufficiently large T. This is a transfer of
a supplied auxiliary counterexample; no such counterexample was obtained.
Ideal closed success does not conversely imply finite-M success at endpoints.

Terra High ran one exact N=5 control over three slopes. The cuts j/k for
k=1,...,4 give 31 cuts and 30 midpoint cells in [0,5). All 4,060 increasing
triples were exhausted. None strictly spoiled every one of the 16 required
row-column pairs. There was no expansion to another N or speed range.
