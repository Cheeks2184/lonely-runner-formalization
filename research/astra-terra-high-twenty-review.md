## Actual task fit and rework

The second Lean cohort handled scalar/orbit helpers, an odd-zero obstruction,
three-even and two-even witness assembly, and the final ordinary/canonical
six-runner bridges. The mathematical contracts were supplied and remained
unchanged. These were proof-engineering tasks, with the larger parity assembly
near the upper end of what should be treated as routine implementation.

Three attempts returned with no check because the worker declined the remaining
assembly or had exhausted its execution/context budget. One retained partial
source. Fresh Terra High contexts and a split at the settled odd-zero interface
then completed the work. This was real orchestration overhead; the returns are
retained rather than counted as successful deliveries or a failure of the goal.

Of the five checked Lean deliveries, four finished with worker-owned repairs.
The two-even assembly reached its two-repair limit and needed direct Astra
proof-engineering repair: 26 added and 15 removed lines, followed by a passing
check. Its record also includes an earlier guidance round. Final independent
source review and the integrated build/trust audit accepted the resulting
canonical N=6 theorem. This does not imply unrestricted LRC is proved.

Receipt quality still needed attention. ThreeEvenOrbit and the first two
TwoEven attempts lacked portions of original source/raw execution evidence;
those historical gaps remain disclosed. The twelve-input Python calculation
passed immediately, but root required recovery of its actual complete driver
and command from the persisted execution transcript. The correction preserved
the original successful output and did not replay the calculation. The next
single-input control retained its complete harness, driver and command before
execution and passed without a correction.

## Continued division of labor

Keep Terra High on cohesive implementations whose mathematical contract and
required dependencies are already settled, and on exact fixed-input checks
using reviewed algorithms. Keep Astra on new mathematics, endpoint/scope
decisions and final independent review. The five checked Lean tasks required
seven worker repair rounds, but only one needed direct Astra edits; this is
consistent with the intended shift of implementation repairs to the worker.

Apply the existing bounded-work rule before reusing a long worker context:
an explicit report of exhausted execution budget calls for a fresh context
with the same High route and a precise interface, not repeated follow-ups into
the exhausted context. Preserve the two-repair cap. Use the already successful
receipt patterns, including complete executed driver bytes, before launching
checks; designing a new capture scheme for each task has caused avoidable work.

Continue accepting complete focused receipts without identical rechecks while
retaining final integration/trust and semantic review. Reassess after thirty
closed High tasks. All twenty usage and total worker-duration fields remain
unknown. Output volume suggests useful implementation offload, but the root's
planning, review and coordination costs prevent a defensible claim about the
amount of Astra usage saved or whether the High setting itself caused any
improvement over the earlier Medium, source-only workflow.
