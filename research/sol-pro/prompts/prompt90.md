## Exact modular pivot data

Fix `n>=2`, put `N=n+1`, and let

```text
a : Fin n -> Nat
```

be positive and injective. Owner labels are indices; never deduplicate labels
when bad subsets coincide. For pivot `j`, put `p=a_j`, `M=N*p`, and define

```text
rho_M(x) = min(x mod M, M-(x mod M)),

R_j = {r : Fin M | N does not divide r},

B_i^j = {r in R_j | rho_M(r*a_i) < p}       for i != j,

Q_j = R_j \ union_(i != j) B_i^j,

F(a) = {j : Fin n | Q_j is empty}.
```

Badness is strict. Equality `rho_M(...)=p` is safe. Retain zero targets, both
cyclic sides, antipodes, nonunits, gcd-degenerate fibers, coincident labelled
sets, and candidate exclusion. Exactly `p` residues modulo `M` are divisible
by `N`, so

```text
|R_j| = M-p = n*p > 0.
```

For every `r in R_j`, the pivot coordinate is safe because

```text
rho_(N*p)(r*p) = p*min(r mod N, N-(r mod N)) >= p.
```

Thus `Q_j` is exactly the finite pivot-certificate set at pivot `j`.

## Complete covers and the distinguished pivot

A labelled owner subset `C subset Fin n \ {j}` is complete at pivot `j` when

```text
R_j = union_(i in C) B_i^j.
```

It is inclusion-minimal when no proper labelled subset is complete. If
`j in F(a)`, the full owner set is complete, so finite deletion produces an
inclusion-minimal complete subcover. Each selected owner then has a nonempty
private set

```text
Priv(j,C,i) = B_i^j \ union_(h in C, h != i) B_h^j.
```

Privacy is only relative to `C`; omitted owners may cover private candidates.

When `F(a)` is nonempty, injectivity gives a unique fastest covered pivot

```text
j_star in F(a),

a_j_star = max {a_j | j in F(a)}.
```

Every other covered pivot `i != j_star` satisfies `a_i<a_j_star`. This strict
order is the new structure; it was absent from the rejected arbitrary-pivot
owner-escape target.

## Sole unrestricted target

There is exactly one target:

```text
MAX-COVERED-PIVOT-INTERNAL-COVER-REDUNDANCY:

For every n>=2 and every positive injective a : Fin n -> Nat,
if F(a) is nonempty and j_star is its unique maximum-speed element, then
for every labelled subset

  C subset F(a) \ {j_star},

if C completely covers R_j_star, there exists i in C such that

  C \ {i}

still completely covers R_j_star.
```

Equivalent minimal-cover form:

```text
Every inclusion-minimal complete cover of R_j_star contains at least one
owner i outside F(a), equivalently Q_i is nonempty.
```

Prove the equivalence of these two forms by finite deletion. The theorem must
quantify over every internal complete cover, not just a response-selected or
lexicographically canonical cover. There is no sparsity bound on `C`.

No height bound, primitive premise, finite-range premise, primality premise,
minimal-counterexample premise, or common-divisor normalization may be added.
Common scaling may be used only after its exact law for `R`, `B`, `Q`, `F`, and
cover minimality is proved.

## Complete implication chain to unrestricted LRC

Assuming the sole target:

1. Suppose a positive injective integer tuple had every pivot covered. Then
   `F(a)=Fin n` is nonempty.
2. Let `j_star` be the global maximum-speed pivot. Since it is covered, its
   full owner set has a finite inclusion-minimal complete subcover `C`.
3. Every owner is in `F(a)`, so `C subset F(a)\{j_star}`.
4. Apply the target. It declares an owner of `C` redundant, contradicting
   inclusion-minimality.
5. Therefore some `Q_j` is nonempty. Select `r in Q_j`; pivot safety and the
   strict complements of all nonpivot bad sets give distance at least `1/N`
   for every coordinate at time `r/(N*a_j)`.
6. This proves positive-integer LRC for `n>=2`; handle `n=1` directly with
   `N=2` and residue `r=1`.
7. Apply the accepted real-to-positive-integer equivalence to obtain
   unrestricted real LRC.

This chain is conditional until the sole target is proved. A finite zero-
failure report or a compiled conditional wrapper does not prove LRC.

## Required mathematical attacks

### Attack A - fastest-covered minimal-cover classification

Assume an inclusion-minimal internal cover `C` exists at `j_star`. Then every
`i in C` is covered and satisfies `a_i<a_j_star`; singleton rigidity also
forces `|C|>=2`. Select exact private candidates

```text
r_i in Priv(j_star,C,i).
```

Classify the centered congruences

```text
r_i*a_i = q_i*N*a_j_star + e_i,   |e_i|<a_j_star,
```

together with safety against every other selected owner. Seek a contradiction
from the simultaneous strict speed order and modular cover conditions. Every
case split must include nonunits, zero targets, both signs of `e_i`, and equal
labelled bad subsets. A private candidate is not globally safe.

### Attack B - exact cross-pivot uncrossing

For each covered owner `i in C`, choose an inclusion-minimal cover at pivot
`i`. Attempt an explicit exchange that replaces `i` in the `j_star` cover by
owner data from the pivot-`i` cover. State the domain, codomain, fibers,
collisions, and strict boundary rule of every residue map. Prove both:

```text
the exchanged owners still cover R_j_star,

and one finite key strictly decreases or one owner outside F(a) appears.
```

Permitted keys include a fully specified lexicographic tuple of cardinality,
maximum owner speed, total owner speed, and increasing owner-speed list. The
key must be frozen before the exchange and proved well-founded. Raw reduction
of a residue modulo another pivot modulus, nearest-boundary selection without
fiber control, or a replacement that can reintroduce a larger covered owner is
not an uncrossing proof.

### Attack C - minimal internal-cover obstruction or counterfamily

Independently test whether an internal minimal cover forces a reusable
arithmetic obstruction: a divisibility cycle, a CRT incompatibility among
private congruences, a strict-product contradiction, or a nonzero determinant
with a proved height-independent bound. The obstruction must use the
`j_star` speed extremality essentially. A determinant that may vanish, a prime
chosen after the tuple, or a bounded-height estimate does not close the target.

If the target is false, prefer a symbolic infinite counterfamily. Otherwise
return the lexicographically first exact counterexample in the mandatory
domain or a precisely declared enlarged domain, including all premise and
cover-minimality checks. Do not weaken the theorem after a failure.
