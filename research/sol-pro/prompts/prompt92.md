
## Sole unrestricted lemma

Prove or refute exactly:

```text

v : Fin n -> {0,...,M_n-1},

  (1) forall k, Delta_k(v)=1,

  (2) forall r : Fin M_n, exists i : Fin n,
        rho_(M_n)(r*v_i) < q_n,

  q_n divides product_(i : Fin n) v_i.
```

The conclusion uses the labelled natural-number product of the canonical
representatives. If any coordinate is zero, it is automatically true; zero
must not be removed from the quantified domain.


classical logic and the exact complement of `<q_n`. Do not add

nondivisibility but is strictly weaker in the reverse direction.

## Strict narrowness relative to PowerForcing

`PowerForcing(n,q_n)` assumes

```text
q_n does not divide v_i  for every i
```

and concludes a common safe `r`. Its cover-form contrapositive would force

```text

```

The aggregate lemma concludes only

```text
q_n divides product_i v_i,
```

which permits the required `E_n` factors of two to be distributed across
several coordinates. Equivalently,

```text

```

is a strictly stronger antecedent than coordinatewise `q_n`-nondivisibility.
Therefore this is a genuine restriction of the PowerForcing domain, not the
same theorem under a product notation.
