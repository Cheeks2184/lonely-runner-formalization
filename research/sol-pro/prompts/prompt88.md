
## Candidate B — continuous Fourier variance gap

Define strict cover multiplicity and its fixed mean

```text
C_a(t) = #{i<n : t in B_i(1/N)},
mu_N = integral C_a = 2*n/N = 2-2/N.
```

If the strict bad sets cover the circle, then integer-valued
`C_a(t)>=1`. Among integer random variables supported on `{1,...,n}` with
mean `mu_N in (1,2)`, convexity gives the sharp variance floor

```text
integral (C_a-mu_N)^2 >= (mu_N-1)*(2-mu_N)
                         = 2*(N-2)/N^2.
```

Parseval rewrites the left side exactly as an infinite additive-energy sum:

```text
sum_{m != 0}
  | sum_{i : a_i divides m}
      sin(2*pi*(m/a_i)/N)/(pi*(m/a_i)) |^2.
```

The proposed sufficient lemma was the universal strict reverse inequality.
It would force an uncovered time without choosing a pivot.

Admission obstruction: it is false far before the LRC frontier. For
`n=3`, `N=4`, `a=(1,2,3)`, exact interval decomposition gives multiplicity
measures

```text
measure(C=1)=2/3,
measure(C=2)=1/6,
measure(C=3)=1/6,
measure(C=0)=0,
```

and hence variance `7/12`, while the cover floor is only `1/4`. There is no
universal upper gap. Truncating frequencies, adding weights, or subtracting a
baseline requires a new exact kernel and a proved inequality; none is frozen.
Candidate B is not launchable.

## Candidate C — third Bonferroni additive-energy gap


The third Bonferroni upper bound is

```text

```

The proposed unrestricted lemma was


restating LRC; choosing a truncation order or a compensating Fourier term is
