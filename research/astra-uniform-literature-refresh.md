# Focused primary-source refresh for the uniform mechanism

Date: 2026-09-06. Root read the primary pages below while the colour-critical
cover argument was active. This is a source-scope check, not a new proof audit,
an exhaustive literature survey, or a reason to pursue successive runner counts.

- [Blanco--Criado--Santos, v2, 27 April 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.24784v2),
  Proposition 1.7, Corollary 1.14 and Theorems 1.16--1.17 distinguish the
  centre-specific minimum from the covering radius. The latter asks for every
  translate and corresponds to the shifted problem. Theorem 1.17 reports a
  shifted counterexample for five moving speeds, hence six total runners and
  a four-dimensional zonotope. The previous local pair-sum note incorrectly
  called this dimension five; its title and count wording are corrected to v2.
  The source was already listed in `docs/literature.md`; this is a refreshed
  scope check, not a newly discovered paper. Its dimension-reduction estimate
  depends on having sufficiently many lattice points and does not replace
  the unresolved bounded-family supply. A periodic/free-phase argument must
  retain the ordinary common-origin restriction. No independent audit of the
  paper's computational certificates was performed here.

- [Perarnau--Serra, v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1407.3381v3), introduction and
  Theorem 6, use a dynamic graph of moving equal-length position intervals.
  Their conclusion is that some runner is alone or four runners are almost
  alone at one time. This is different from the present static graph of
  strict bad-time arcs and does not supply the designated runner's full
  witness. The source also gives correlation-based weaker bounds. None of
  these statements closes the current ordinary supply obligation.

- [Jensen, v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27941), abstract, describes an exact
  two-speed mixed-threshold characterization and unequal-threshold integral
  formulas. This source was already considered in the project's strict-to-mixed
  audit. The abstract supplies no arbitrary-count sharp witness theorem;
  it is not being introduced as a new uniform induction result.

The focused search also surfaced fixed-count computer-assisted work and the
previously screened Riesz-product route. Those results do not justify returning
to a runner-count ladder or relaunching a rejected product-supply condition.
No additional theorem is adopted, no dependency restored, and no mathematical
computation run. The next novel argument still needs an actual uniform supply
constraint, with closed endpoints and zero initial phases retained.
